[Pitch]
Rocjob is an HD-2D, rhythm-driven beat 'em up: a love letter to Streets of Rage and Turtles in Time. You time-travel through rock history and play as the band members themselves, in local 2-player co-op, fighting to the bands' songs. Every stage is built from a band's legend, in painted pixel-art worlds you can almost hear.


[In the download]
- Windows build: unzip it and run Rocjob.exe. If it does not start, run Play_Compatibility.bat.
- Mac build
- README_FRIENDS.txt: a short how-to-play for friends
- RELEASE_NOTES.txt: what changed in this build
- No Linux build yet: the Windows build runs under Wine.


[Features]
- 2-player local co-op beat 'em up; 22 playable band members across five bands: Thin Lizzy, Guns N' Roses, Pantera, The Smashing Pumpkins and Radiohead
- Combat that lives on the beat: time your hits to the music for bigger combos
- 18 stages across three acts, from Dublin streets to a Y2K finale, plus bosses, mini-bosses and crossover stages where the bands team up
- Guitar solos as quick-time moments, and FULL SHRED: a guitar-hero-style note highway you play along to
- Painted pixel-art worlds with a cinematic lighting look (an optional ULTRA LOOK setting adds depth, glow and atmosphere)
- Controller support for many pad types (Xbox, PlayStation, Switch, Saturn and Mega Drive style pads) and keyboard, with on-screen prompts that match your device
- Settings for quality, difficulty and accessibility

[How to play]
Move with the stick or keys, hit on the beat, grab, jump and use your special. Two players share one screen. Full controls are in the game's Settings and in README_FRIENDS.txt in the download.
